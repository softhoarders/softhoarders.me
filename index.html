About Us

Who we are and what we stand for

Soft Hoarders (FTC Team 12560, RO031) is a nonprofit student robotics team based at "Frații Buzești" National College in Craiova, Romania. Since 2016, we have competed in FIRST Tech Challenge — a global program that teaches engineering, programming, teamwork, and community leadership through real-world robot design and competition.

01

Educational mission

We are not a commercial organization. Our work is educational and community-focused: we build robots, mentor younger students, run STEM workshops, and represent Romania at international FTC events. Every season, our students learn skills that prepare them for careers in engineering, software, design, and science — while giving back to the community that supports us.

02

Open collaboration

Our team operates under the guidance of faculty mentors and is supported by sponsors who believe in youth STEM education. We publish our activities openly, welcome collaboration with schools and organizations, and invite anyone interested in robotics or STEM outreach to get in touch.

Programs & Services

How we serve our community

Beyond building competition robots, Soft Hoarders runs ongoing STEM education and outreach programs for students, schools, and the public in Craiova and beyond.

01

Competitive Robotics (FTC)

Each season, our students design, build, program, and compete with a fully autonomous and tele-operated robot. Through FTC, members develop engineering, CAD, software, and project management skills while learning Gracious Professionalism and Coopertition — core FIRST values.

02

STEM Workshops & Demos

We organize hands-on workshops and robot demonstrations for elementary and middle school students, introducing them to mechanics, coding, and problem-solving. In the 2024–2025 season alone, we participated in 57+ outreach events across Romania.

03

School & Community Partnerships

We collaborate with local schools, libraries, and community organizations to bring robotics and STEM activities to audiences who might not otherwise have access. We welcome invitations to present at fairs, open days, and educational events.

04

Mentorship & Team Development

Senior team members mentor new recruits through a structured onboarding process covering mechanical assembly, Java programming, 3D design, and marketing. Our mentors — teachers from Frații Buzești College — provide ongoing academic and technical guidance.
